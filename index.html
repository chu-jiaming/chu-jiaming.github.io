Research

I'm interested in generative models, 3D reconstruction, and world models. My current work studies how radiance fields behave when their inputs, such as camera poses, are imperfect, and how to evaluate them so that the metrics measure what we think they measure.

PoseGuard-NeRF: When Does Joint Camera-Pose Optimization Help NeRF, and When Does It Just Move the World?
Jiaming Chu
Independent research project, 2026
project page / code / release v1.0.0

A controlled study of 45 vanilla-NeRF runs (9 pose-noise levels × 5 methods): a camera learning-rate warm-up lets joint pose optimization recover most of the quality lost to noisy poses (14.1 → 22.7 dB at 5% translation noise), yet enabling it from perfect poses drops PSNR from 27.1 to 17.9 dB. A Sim(3) fit on training cameras alone recovers +5.5 dB, revealing global coordinate drift rather than a worse reconstruction.
