Research
VGGT-Splat: Feed-forward VGGT Poses vs. COLMAP for 3D Gaussian Splatting, from 8 Frames to All
Jiaming Chu
Independent research project, 2026
project page / code

A controlled experiment on 6 scenes × 8 / 16 / 32 / all frames: with few frames COLMAP fails on most scenes while VGGT never does; with all frames COLMAP leads by 2.1–4.6 dB on five of six scenes, likely because VGGT's poses are less precise, not because it covers less. A VGGT depth prior reshapes the geometry but barely changes novel views, since the few-frame bottleneck is unseen regions.

PoseGuard-NeRF: When Does Joint Camera-Pose Optimization Help NeRF, and When Does It Just Move the World?
Jiaming Chu
Independent research project, 2026
project page / code

A controlled experiment with 45 vanilla-NeRF runs (9 pose-noise levels × 5 methods): a camera learning-rate warm-up lets joint pose optimization recover much of the quality lost to noisy poses (14.1 → 22.7 dB at 5% translation noise), yet enabling naive pose optimization from perfect poses drops PSNR from 27.1 to 17.9 dB. A Sim(3) fitted on training cameras alone recovers +5.5 dB, showing that most of the drop is global coordinate drift rather than a worse reconstruction.

AFVE-Bench: Measuring Abandoned-Frame Verbalization Errors in Multi-Turn LLM Revision
Jiaming Chu
Preprint, 2026
arXiv

When a user replaces an earlier frame mid-dialogue, a revision can adopt the new target yet still expose the abandoned one. A benchmark of 192 matched four-turn English and Chinese dialogues, with 4,204 doubly annotated responses from 11 model configurations: 25.5% of history-conditioned responses commit this error, and 19.8% of the ones that pass the target still verbalize the old frame, mostly through contrastive framing.
